## About me

M.Sc. Spatial Planning student at **TU Dortmund** and Research Assistant on the EU-funded **[AquaINFRA](https://aquainfra.eu/)** project at **[Hochschule Bochum](https://www.hochschule-bochum.de/)**. I turn spatial data into planning decisions with GIS, Python and R, and build reproducible, FAIR-compliant geospatial workflows.

- 🗺️ Spatial analysis: accessibility models, spatial autocorrelation, geospatial mapping
- 🌊 FAIR & open data: reproducible research workflows and open educational resources
- 🐳 Geoprocessing pipelines: Docker, R spatial packages, Python for cleaning and statistics
